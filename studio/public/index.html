    Operator guide

    One approved foundation, two production workflows

    Complete sources, sync, and transcript once. Then choose Full Episode or Social Clips. Each branch keeps its own approvals and outputs. Uploading and publishing happen outside Studio.

    Before your first episode Set up this computer once

    Edit show.config.json for your show name, host names and links, then save and approve the reusable show opening, closing, and CTA defaults.
